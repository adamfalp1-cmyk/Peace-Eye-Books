ABOUT

Founded in 2026 by cartoonist and painter Adam Falp, Peace Eye is an independent shop dedicated to underground comix, alternative comics and unusual printed matter. Falp has spent more than a decade making counter culture inspired Art, including his own titles Electric Chair and Smokin' Bedroom.

Peace Eye grew out of that same enthusiasm for comics and the people who make them. Its shelves bring together the American undergrounds of the 1960s and '70s, contemporary independent cartoonists, old science-fiction paperbacks, fanzines, and underground ephemera.

Peace Eye continues the tradition of the old school comics shop, inviting comix fans to dig through long boxes, meet one another, and discover strange printed matter.
